How it works

Three steps. None of them are yours.

01

We set up on your par-3

Our staffer, our banner, our tablet, our gift card float. We check in with the pro shop, run the hole, and pack out. Your team does nothing.

02

Golfers opt in for $20

Cash or card, as each group reaches the tee. It takes under a minute and doesn't slow play. About half of everyone we talk to plays.

03

Hit the green, double your money

Green in regulation wins a $40 gift card, redeemable in your pro shop, clubhouse or toward a future round. A hole-in-one wins $1,000.

We fund every prize — including the $1,000. Your course carries no prize liability and no staffing cost. You sell us gift cards at face value and your shop gets the redemption traffic.

Outings & tournaments

Charity scrambles, corporate days, member-guests. We bring the whole challenge as a hole sponsor — banner, staff, gift cards and the $1,000 hole-in-one — so your organisers get a marquee hole without organising anything.

Opt-in runs higher at outings

For directors of golf

The questions you're actually asking.

What does my course actually have to do?

Operationally, nothing. We bring the staffer, the banner, the tablet and the gift card float. We check in with your pro shop when we arrive, run the hole, and pack out at the end of the shift. You don't assign staff, you don't handle money, and you don't manage prizes.

Who pays the $1,000 if someone makes a hole-in-one?

We do. Girdie Golf funds 100% of prizes, including the $1,000 hole-in-one. It has happened once so far and we paid it in full, same day. Your course carries no prize liability whatsoever.

How does the course make money?

You sell us gift cards at face value for every winner. In 2026 that came to $112,360 across our 11 partner courses — an average of about $702 for every event day we ran. Winners then redeem those cards in your pro shop, clubhouse, or toward a future round, which brings them through your door and usually gets them spending above the card value.

Do you take a cut of anything?

No. There is no revenue split — not with your course, not with anyone. You sell us gift cards at face value and that money is yours. We keep the entry fees and we pay for the prizes out of them. Two clean transactions, no percentages, no invoicing back and forth.

Will this slow down play?

No. The challenge happens as groups arrive at a hole they were already going to play. Opting in takes under a minute, and the shot is the tee shot they were about to hit anyway. We've run 160 event days without a pace-of-play complaint.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry a standard general liability policy, and every course we operate at is named as an additional insured on it. Ask and we'll send your certificate over before we ever set foot on the property.

Which hole works best?

A par-3 with a clear view of the green, ideally somewhere with natural traffic like near the turn. We'll walk the course with you and recommend one — it usually takes about fifteen minutes to work out.

Can you do a one-off outing instead?

Yes, and we do a lot of them — charity scrambles, corporate days, member-guests. We come in as a hole sponsor and run the whole thing. Your event doesn't need to be at one of our partner courses.

How long is the trial, and what happens after?

One weekend, no contract and no minimum. Afterward we'll show you the numbers from your own course: golfers engaged, opt-in rate, gift cards sold. If it doesn't work for you, that's the end of it and there's nothing to cancel.
